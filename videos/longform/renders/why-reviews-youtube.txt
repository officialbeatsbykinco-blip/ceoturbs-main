Why the Worse Business Gets All the Customers

Two bakeries on the same street. One has a line out the door. The other is empty, and it bakes better bread. Why does almost everyone walk past it? The answer is one little number on your phone, and the research behind it is wild: 97% of people read reviews, almost half won't even try a business with fewer than 20, and the first 5 reviews can make a product 270% more likely to sell.

This is the story of Pip, a hedgehog baker with the best croissants in town and only 4 reviews, and what he did about it.
