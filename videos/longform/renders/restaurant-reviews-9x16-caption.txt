Her food was better. The place across the street was packed. Here's why 👇

Hungry people pick a restaurant on Google before they taste anything. They look at the stars, then at how many reviews you have.

4.9 stars with 23 reviews looks like a place nobody goes. 4.7 stars with 612 looks like the safe bet. 🤔

The fix: one tap card on every table. A guest holds their phone near it and your Google review page opens. About 10 seconds. Ask right when they say "that was amazing," then walk away. (And no free dessert for reviews. Google doesn't allow it.)
