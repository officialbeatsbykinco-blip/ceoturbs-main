# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local business owners, office managers, and Tampa-area homeowners arriving from search and paid ads. They want a fast, low-friction way to get a price, book setup or repair work, or explain what they need without navigating a call center.

## Product Purpose

CEO Turbo is a direct-response marketing site for Tampa tech help, office setup, and growth services. It turns ad and search traffic into qualified conversations and booked Cal.com appointments. Success means the primary action matches the visitor's device: desktop visitors can schedule directly, while phone visitors can answer a few questions and send the request by text.

## Brand Personality

Direct, practical, hands-on. The site should feel like dealing with the person who will do the work: clear prices, no fluff, fast answers, and a confident next step.

## Anti-references

Do not make desktop visitors hunt for a phone or text workflow. Do not lead desktop ad traffic into SMS links or mobile-only question flows. Do not bury the Cal.com booking action behind vague contact language, generic agency polish, or unnecessary forms.

## Design Principles

- Match the conversion path to the device and intent.
- Make the next action obvious within the first screen.
- Show the real work, prices, service area, and expectations plainly.
- Keep the voice human, specific, and accountable.
- Preserve usable fallbacks when embeds or scripts load slowly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Keep keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, labeled form fields, and touch targets of at least 44px. Device-specific behavior must have a usable non-JavaScript fallback.
