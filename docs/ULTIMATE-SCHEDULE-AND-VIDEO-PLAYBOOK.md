# CEO Turbo — Ultimate Schedule + Video Playbook

Goal: Google review cards (attract) → setup/Brand Boost call (core) → monthly retainer (continuity). Office transformation runs separately on ads.

## What the research says (and what it doesn't)

Verified review behavior (BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2026, 1,002 US adults, Feb 2026):
- 97% of consumers read online reviews; 41% say they *always* do (up from 29%).
- 74% look for reviews from the last 3 months; 32% want them from the last 2 weeks.
- 31% will only use businesses rated 4.5 stars or higher (up from 17%).
- 80% are more likely to use a business that replies to all reviews; generic templated replies put off 50%.
- Google is still the top review site, but its share fell to 71% (from 83%); people use about 6 review sites.

Video guidance (vendor blogs, mostly weak sourcing — treat as direction, not proof):
- The first 1–3 seconds decide distribution; use an open loop (curiosity gap, contrarian claim, number, direct callout).
- Sound-off viewing is common: burn captions into every video.
- Authentic, phone-native, under ~30–60 seconds beats polished brand films at the top of the funnel.
- TikTok = cold reach, Reels = conversion with existing followers, YouTube Shorts = search-driven and long-lived.

**Gap:** no source had conversion data for NFC review-card videos. Your own numbers are the only real answer. Tag every bio link with `?utm_source=<platform>&utm_content=<video-id>` and keep a log.

## Rules before the schedule

Rank every activity by expected dollars per hour. Do the top of the list first, the bottom last:
1. Closing calls and demos (money now)
2. In-person demos with a card in hand (highest close rate)
3. Follow-ups with people who already replied or watched
4. Cold email (runs while you work — use a separate sending domain, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, a real address and an unsubscribe line)
5. Cold calls to business numbers + DMs
6. Content (slow, but compounds and makes everything above easier)
7. Ads management

Cold SMS: skip unless the person replied or opted in (TCPA risk, carrier blocking).
Content volume: 2–3 good videos a day, not 10. 10 rushed videos burn you out before the data arrives.
Honest sleep note: 6 hours is the floor. If you notice the afternoon block collapsing, move bedtime to 11:30.

## Weekly structure

- **Monday:** Plan + outreach heavy; batch-film the week's hardest video.
- **Tuesday–Thursday:** Full field sales + calls (best days for owners to take meetings).
- **Friday:** Demo/setup calls and fulfillment; send weekly client reports.
- **Saturday:** Barbers/salons are slammed — walk in before 11 AM, then film in the afternoon.
- **Sunday:** Half day. Review the scoreboard, pick the winning hook, batch-animate next week, 4+ hours fully off.
