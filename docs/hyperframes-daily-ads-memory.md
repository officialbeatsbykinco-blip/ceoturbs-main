# HyperFrames Daily Ads Memory - Google Review Cards

**Product:** CEO Turbo Google Review Cards
**URL:** https://www.ceoturbo.com/google-review-cards
**Goal:** High-converting animated ads for social media (Shorts, Meta Feed/Reels, TikTok)

---

## AD FORMAT SPECIFICATIONS

### Video Sizing
- **Shorts/Reels/TikTok:** 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical)
- **Safe Zones:**
  - Top 300px: Empty (profile info)
  - Bottom 400px: Empty (CTA overlays)
  - Content zone: Y=300px to Y=1520px ONLY

### Animation Style
- **Primary:** Stick figures or animated animals
- **Tone:** Bold, emotional, make business owners feel the pain of NOT having it
- **Voice:** High quality voice animation (HyperFrames voice skills)
- **Quality:** No slop, premium animations, smooth transitions

### Compliance Rules
- ❌ No "guaranteed" claims
- ❌ No unrealistic income promises
- ❌ No government-issued claims
- ✅ Bold emotional triggers allowed
- ✅ Make them feel the pain of missing out
- ✅ Strong urgency without deception
