## 1. Strategy & Mindset

HVAC business owners and operators work in the field, run dispatch, or manage technicians in attics and crawlspaces. 

### Why Standard Cold Outreach Fails with HVAC
* **Too wordy:** Long agency paragraphs get deleted before the second sentence.
* **Corporate jargon:** Words like "synergy," "scaling," "digital transformation," or "demo" trigger immediate spam deletion.
* **Shared lead fatigue:** They already burn thousands on Angi, Thumbtack, and HomeAdvisor where 5 contractors race to underbid each other.

### What Actually Works
1. **Short & direct:** 3 sentences max for SMS; under 90 words for email.
2. **Focus on high-ticket jobs:** System changeouts, replacements, and off-season maintenance agreements.
3. **Low-friction foot in the door:** The NFC Google Review Card for field technicians to capture 5-star reviews before pulling out of the customer's driveway.
4. **Timing:** 
   * **Morning Window:** 7:00 AM – 8:30 AM (before the first service call).
   * **Evening Window:** 4:30 PM – 6:30 PM (wrapping up tickets/invoices in the truck).

- [ ] **Morning (7:30 AM – 8:30 AM):** Send 5–10 personalized SMS to owners in trucks.
- [ ] **Mid-day (11:00 AM – 1:00 PM):** Send personalized cold emails with lowercase subject lines.
- [ ] **Late Afternoon (4:30 PM – 6:00 PM):** Process replies. Send 2-day follow-ups for non-responders.

  1. *For Reviews:* "Awesome! Here's a 30-sec look at the card: ceoturbo.com/google-review-cards. What's the best address to mail you a sample?"
  2. *For Jobs:* "Great. What area do you prefer working in most, and how many extra install/replacement calls could your crews comfortably handle per week?"
