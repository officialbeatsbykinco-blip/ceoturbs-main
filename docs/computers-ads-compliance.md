## Ad wording
- Use: "in person", "we come to you", "computer repair", "setup", "upgrades", "price before we start", "call or text".
- Avoid: "tech support", "help desk", "virus", "pop-up", "scam", "hotline", "guaranteed", "best", "#1", "fastest", "certified", "official", any brand name (Microsoft, Windows, Apple, Dell, HP).
- Do not use "Geek Squad", "Best Buy" or any competitor name in ad text.
