"Quick thing: when the cards arrive, do you know where you'll put them and who'll hand them out? The businesses that get the most reviews hand them out at checkout, every customer, no exceptions. I can send you a one-page how-to."

If no: "No problem. I'll text the how-to and check in after your first 10 reviews."

Rules: never push a second upsell in the same call. Always send a text after. Log who said "not now" and call back in 14 days.
