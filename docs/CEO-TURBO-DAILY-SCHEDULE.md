# CEO Turbo: Daily Job Schedule (repeat every day)

Goal: book 1 sales call or sell 1 review card/GBP setup every day, while DoorDash pays the bills.

## Daily numbers (the quota)

| Channel | Per day | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| New leads pulled | 40 | Running ads, or under 50 Google reviews |
| SMS | 40 (one to one, typed or pasted, not blasted) | Same 40 leads, sent first |
| Calls | 60 dials (the 40 + 20 callbacks) | Same day as the SMS |
| Email | 40 personalized + the mass list at 8:45am | Sent after the call, so they see you 3 times |
| Walk-ins | 10-15 | 2:00-4:30pm only |
| Content | 1 long, 1 short, 1 Hyperframes animation | Batch edit, post on a schedule |

Compliance: only text a business number with a relevant, specific message, include "reply STOP to opt out", and honor it right away. Include your name, address, and an unsubscribe link in emails. Do not use autodialers or bulk SMS tools on cold numbers.

## What to pitch (the ladder)

Everyone gets one hook first, not the whole menu.

1. **Hook (free): the Leak Audit.** "I looked at where your ad clicks go. Here are 3 things that cost you customers." This works because they already pay for traffic.
2. **Entry sale ($67-$147):** Google review card or GBP setup. Easy yes, fast result.
3. **Core sale ($297+):** a landing page or website that converts the clicks they already pay for.
4. **Recurring ($35-$297/mo):** Care, Growth, or Scale plans, plus ad management.

If they run ads, lead with the audit and the landing page. If they do not, lead with reviews and GBP.

## Cold call script

**Opener (10 sec):**
"Hey [Name], it's [You] with CEO Turbo. I know I'm calling out of the blue. I texted you this morning about your ad. Got 30 seconds, and if it's not relevant you can hang up?"

**Reason (15 sec):**
"I clicked your ad for [service]. I noticed [specific leak]. People who land there and can't [call/book/trust you in 5 seconds] leave and call the next one. I fix exactly that."

**Question (the hinge):**
"Are the leads from your ads where you want them to be right now, or are you paying for clicks that don't turn into calls?"

*Stop. Let them talk. Ask one follow-up: "How many calls a week are you getting from it?"*

**Offer:**
"What I'd do is send you a free audit showing the 3 biggest leaks. If it makes sense, I fix them for $[X] and you start seeing more calls. Can I send it now? What's the best email?"

**Close:**
"I can walk you through it in 10 minutes. Does [tomorrow 10am] or [tomorrow 3pm] work better?"

**Objections:**
- *"Not interested."* "Totally fair. Just so I don't bug you again: are you happy with how many customers your ads bring in?"
- *"I already have a website person."* "Great. I'm not replacing them. I'll send the audit, and you can hand it to them."
- *"How much?"* "Starts at $67 for review cards and $297 for the page. Audit is free, so you see the problem first."
- *"Send me info."* "Sure. What's the best email? I'll make it specific to your business, not a generic PDF. If it helps, I'll call Thursday."
- *"No budget for ads."* "That's why I start with reviews and your Google profile. They cost nothing per click."

**Voicemail (15 sec):** "Hey [Name], [You] from CEO Turbo. I texted about your Google ad. I found 3 things losing you customers. I'll email the list in a minute. Call me at [number]."

## In-person (2:00-4:30pm)

1. Bring a sample review card with their business name on it.
2. "Hey, I'm local. I noticed you have [X] reviews and [competitor] has [Y]. I make a tap-to-review card. Let me show you." Tap it on your phone.
3. Close: "$[67-97], done by [day]. Want one for the counter?"
4. Leave the card and a business card. Follow up the next day.

## Content (rules)

- Long form: one real example daily ("I audited [public business]'s Google presence and here's what's costing them calls"). Use real proof only. No made-up numbers or testimonials.
- Short: the best 30 seconds of the long form.
- Hyperframes animation: one visual of the day's lesson. Put your call link on every post.
- Topic comes from the day's calls: whatever objection you heard most.

## Tracking (end of day)

Leads pulled / texts / dials / conversations / emails / walk-ins / audits sent / calls booked / sales. If 1-2 sales per day is not happening after 2 weeks, change the offer or the list, not the effort.
