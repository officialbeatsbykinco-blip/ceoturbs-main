# Google Ads: mobile car battery + jump start

Landing page: https://www.ceoturbo.com/car-battery
Prices on page: jump start from $59, installed battery from $169, fleet from $149/vehicle (edit in src/content/pricing.ts).

## Setup
- Campaign type: Search, goal Calls + Leads. Call ads and call asset ON with (678) 558-4327, call reporting ON.
- Location: Duluth/Atlanta, 40 mile radius, "Presence" only (people in the area).
- Schedule: all hours (batteries die at night too) if you can answer; otherwise 6am-11pm.
- Bidding: start Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks with a CPC cap, switch to Maximize Conversions after ~15 calls.
- Start budget: what you can answer. Calls you miss are wasted money.
- Conversion: count calls over 30 seconds.

## Ad groups and keywords (phrase and exact)
1. Dead battery: "car battery replacement near me", "mobile battery replacement", "car won't start battery", "dead car battery help"
2. Jump start: "jump start near me", "mobile jump start", "car jump start service"

## Before you turn it on
- Have the batteries in stock for the common sizes (group 24F, 35, 51R, 65, 78, 94R/H7, 48/H6) and a jump pack and tester.
- Check with a mechanic friend which sizes cover most cars you'll see, and what your cost is, so $169 leaves margin.
- Get general liability insurance for working on customers' vehicles.
- Do NOT claim a response time or a warranty unless you can honor it.
- Check Google's current policy for roadside/auto services in the Ads policy center before launch.
